Your learning stays yours.

This policy explains how the ShiurNotes web app and Shiur AI Chrome extension handle information when creating notes or transcripts.

The Chrome extension

The extension stores your Gemini API key, generated notes, preferences, and usage information locally in your browser. The developer cannot see your key or your saved library. When you process a shiur, your browser sends the required audio directly to Google’s Gemini API.

Extension data is local to your browser. You can clear your API key or delete notes from the extension’s Settings page at any time.

The ShiurNotes web app

When you submit a supported shiur URL, the service temporarily retrieves the associated audio and sends it to the configured AI provider so it can create the requested notes or transcript. The service may temporarily cache generated results to improve reliability and avoid repeated processing.

Information we handle

  • The shiur URL you submit and the audio associated with it.
  • The notes or transcript produced from that audio.
  • Basic technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

We do not sell personal information, use your learning for advertising, or request your Google password.

Third-party services

Audio and prompts are processed by Google’s Gemini service. YUTorah, Kol Halashon, ShiurBank, Google, and your hosting provider may handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

You may stop using either service at any time. In the extension, you can delete individual notes, clear your entire library, clear your API key, or uninstall the extension.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to shiurnotes@yahoo.com.
